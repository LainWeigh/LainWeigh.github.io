Xiaoyi Wei「魏潇逸」

Hello there! I am a PhD candidate in College of Intelligent Robotics and Advanced Manufacturing, Fudan University, advised by Prof. Lihua Zhang.

I received my bachelor's degree from Intelligent Science and Technology at Fudan University.

My research interests include whole-body control, reinforcement learning, and loco-manipulation.

If you are interested in any of these topics, or would simply like to chat, feel free to contact me :)

KiRAS: Keyframe Guided Self-Imitation for Robust and Adaptive Skill Learning in Quadruped Robots

Xiaoyi Wei, Peng Zhai, Jiaxin Tu, Yueqi Zhang, Yuqi Li, Zonghao Zhang, Hu Zhou, Lihua Zhang

International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2026

[Project Page] [Paper] [Video]

KiRAS uses single-keyframe guided self-imitation to represent multiple quadruped skills within one policy, enabling robust and adaptive terrain traversal.
Continuous Control of Diverse Skills in Quadruped Robots Without Complete Expert Datasets

Jiaxin Tu*, Xiaoyi Wei*, Yueqi Zhang, Taixian Hou, Xiaofei Gao, Zhiyan Dong, Peng Zhai, Lihua Zhang

International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2025

[Paper] [Video] [BibTeX]

This work applies GASIL to quadruped skill learning, represents each skill with a single keyframe, and enables an 8-DoF robot to learn bipedal walking and locomotion at different base heights.
Robust Proximal Adversarial Reinforcement Learning Under Model Mismatch

Peng Zhai, Xiaoyi Wei, Taixian Hou, Xiaopeng Ji, Zhiyan Dong, Jiafu Yi, Lihua Zhang

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), 2024

[Paper] [BibTeX]

This work improves RL robustness under model mismatch with a lightweight adversarial framework that uses short-term rewards and PGD-generated perturbations, and demonstrates strong sim-to-real transfer on legged robots and quadrupeds.

 Music-Driven Legged Robots: Synchronized Walking to Rhythmic Beats

Taixian Hou, Yueqi Zhang, Xiaoyi Wei, Zhiyan Dong, Jiafu Yi, Peng Zhai, Lihua Zhang

International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2025

[Project Page] [Paper] [BibTeX]

This work combines a trainable phase tracker, oscillators, and a phase modulator to generate diverse gaits and synchronize quadruped locomotion with musical rhythms in real time.
MUJICA: Multi-skill Unified Joint Integration of Control Architecture for Wheeled-Legged Robots

Yuqi Li, Peng Zhai, Yueqi Zhang, Xiaoyi Wei, Quancheng Qian, Zhengxu He, Qianxiang Yu, Lihua Zhang

International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2026

[Project Page] [Paper] [Video]

MUJICA unifies diverse wheeled-legged skills in a proprioceptive policy with a learned skill selector, enabling robust transitions and adaptive traversal in complex environments.
Reinforcement Learning-Based Algorithm for Real-Time Automated Parking Decision Making

Xiaoyi Wei, Taixian Hou, Xiao Zhao, Jiaxin Tu, Haiyang Guan, Peng Zhai, Lihua Zhang

CAAI International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, 2023

[Paper] [BibTeX]

This work combines curriculum learning with SAC for real-time automated parking, enabling vehicles to learn parking and obstacle avoidance in unregulated lots with narrow spaces and random obstacles.
A3E2 Net: Integrating Waypoints, Lanes, and Traces for Multi-Modal Trajectory Prediction

Yuqi Li*, Xiaoyi Wei*, Xiao Zhao, Peng Zhai, Lihua Zhang

International Conference on Tools with Artificial Intelligence (ICTAI), 2025

[Paper] [BibTeX]

A3E2Net fuses waypoints, lanes, and motion traces with cross-attention and goal-driven autoregressive decoding for accurate and diverse multi-modal trajectory prediction.
